# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Heimdall to be a welcoming project for everyone. We pledge to make participation free of harassment for every
person, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that help an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Show empathy and kindness to other people.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it with grace.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to the people they affect, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the whole community, not only for one person.

Examples of behavior that we do not accept:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit
  permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They take fair and appropriate action in response to any behavior
that they think is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct. They give the reasons for moderation decisions when that is appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and chat. It
also applies when a person represents the project in public spaces, for example with an official project account or as
a named representative at an event.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers use these steps to decide the consequence of an action:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional or unwelcome behavior. The consequence is a
   private, written warning that explains the problem. A public apology can be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions. The consequence is a warning, and no interaction
   with the people involved for a stated period. A violation of these terms can lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior. The consequence is a
   temporary ban from all interaction with the community for a stated period.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression toward or disparagement of
   groups of people. The consequence is a permanent ban from all public interaction with the community.
