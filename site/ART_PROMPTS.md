# Heimdall art pack, round 3

Two ideas from your references, in one world:

1. **The Bifröst:** a rainbow bridge from Heimdall's feet to a golden Asgard. This is the merge path, branch to `main`.
2. **The realms:** small glass worlds circling Asgard on thin golden orbits. These are the tools Heimdall connects to (GitHub, Jira, chat). **Leave the orbs blank.** The website puts the real icons on them in code, so they stay sharp and correct.

Keep it bright: golden hour, light sky. The website is light, so a dark, stormy image will clash with it.

## Where to generate

- **Krea** (krea.ai): 16:9, the largest size. Use the same seed for every prompt to keep the style.
- **Gemini (Nano Banana) / Google Flow:** best for edits ("make the bridge brighter") on an image you like.
- **Midjourney:** add `--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 300`.

## Style line (start every prompt with this)

> Luminous painterly fantasy concept art, soft oil and watercolour brushwork, warm golden-hour light, airy pale sky with soft cream and lavender clouds, gentle atmospheric haze, rich but soft colours, calm and majestic, high detail, clean composition. No text, no letters, no logos, no symbols, no watermark.

## Heimdall (paste this unchanged each time)

> Heimdall, an original Norse guardian: long pale-gold hair, a simple golden winged helm with no horns, a deep crimson cloak with fine gold embroidery, a long golden horn (Gjallarhorn) held at his side. Seen from behind or in three-quarter view, looking at the bridge. Not based on any film, game or comic character.

The spheres are plain and have no markings.** A faint golden tree of light rises far behind the clouds on the right. **Keep the upper left 45% of the image as calm, open, pale sky with soft clouds and nothing else.** Headline text goes there.

## Edit prompts (in Gemini or Krea, with the image attached)

- "Make the rainbow bridge wider, brighter and more solid, like glass you can walk on. Keep everything else the same."
- "Remove all marks, letters and symbols from the glass spheres. Keep them plain."
- "Make the sky in the upper left lighter and emptier. Remove the clouds there."
- "Make the whole image a little brighter and warmer, golden hour."

## Do not

- Do not put logos or letters in the art. The site adds the real icons.
- Do not use a dark, stormy sky. Keep it light and golden.
- Do not draw a horned helmet or game-style armour.
- Do not use stock images. They have watermarks and licences.
